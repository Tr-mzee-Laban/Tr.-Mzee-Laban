About Me

A short introduction

My name is Tr. Mzee Laban Kanyesigye. I am a teacher, reading and writing facilitator, educational mentor, and author of learning and life-guidance materials. My work focuses on helping learners build strong foundations in reading, spelling, phonics, comprehension, and writing, while encouraging teachers and families to support every child's progress.

I believe that patient guidance, consistent practice, discipline, and a positive attitude can open doors to a better future.
